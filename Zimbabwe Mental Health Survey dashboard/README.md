# Zimbabwe Mental Health Survey Dashboard

## Overview

This repository contains a visual dashboard summarizing results from a mental health survey conducted in Zimbabwe. The dashboard presents key insights on the mental health status, barriers to access, and demographic profiles of survey respondents.

## Dashboard Features

- **Survey Respondents:** Displays total number of respondents (60).
- **Demographics:**
  - Gender distribution (85% Female, 15% Male)
  - Age groups ranging from 18 to 40+ years
  - Marital status (Single, Married, Other)
  - Occupation breakdown (Formally employed, Unemployed, Student, Self-employed)
- **Mental Health Status:**
  - Current status by gender: Stable, Not great, Would like help, Need help
  - Barriers to accessing mental health help: Financial, Lack of facilities, Social pressure, Other, Religious reasons
- **Stress and Financials:**
  - Main causes of stress among respondents
  - Proposed session fee per hour (USD): Average and distribution
- **Data Visualization:**
  - Pie charts, bar charts, and line plots for intuitive understanding
  - Clear legend and category breakdowns for each survey dimension

## Usage

- Share research findings on Zimbabwe mental health needs with stakeholders, NGOs, and policymakers.
- Highlight most pressing challenges: financial barriers, lack of facilities, and social stigma.
- Support advocacy and data-driven program development.
